## Capture rules

- Capture the existing interface directly; do not recreate it as a graphic or modify application source to stage footage.
- Do not record a human decision submission or change case data for the video.
- Never portray local simulated agent events as live Switch activity.
- Do not claim invoice documents were inspected beyond the synthetic fields shown in the app.
- Keep sensitive credentials out of frame; Switch settings are backend-only and are not needed for this walkthrough.
